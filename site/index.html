MacGet

A free, open-source download manager for macOS. Up to 16 parallel threads per file.

Download for macOS
Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later · Free forever · MIT licensed
Full site, documentation, and install guide: macget.suryansh.work
  • Multi-threadedHTTP Range requests across up to 16 parallel connections per file.
  • Pause / ResumeState survives quit-and-relaunch. Restart your Mac, pick up where you left off.
  • NativeBuilt in SwiftUI. Looks and behaves like a Mac app should.
  • FreeNo ads, no telemetry, no in-app purchases. Open source on GitHub.
